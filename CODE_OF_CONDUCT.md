# Code of Conduct

Ihsaanly exists to help people practise their faith with ihsan: excellence and
sincerity. We ask the same of how we treat each other here.

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and anywhere you represent the project. It's adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which applies in full where this summary is silent.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their
  second language.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Accept correction gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Respect differences of opinion, including between schools of thought.
  Where scholars differ, the app shows the difference rather than taking
  sides, and so do we.

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sectarian attacks, takfir, or mocking anyone's faith or practice, or lack
  of it.
- Hateful or demeaning comments about anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality,
  gender, age, disability or appearance.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or derailing a discussion on purpose.

## Reporting

Email <support@ihsaanly.app> with "Code of Conduct" in the subject. Tell us
what happened, where, and who was involved; links help. Reports are handled
confidentially by the maintainers, and anyone named in a report won't take
part in handling it.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor,
depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is. We'll tell the
reporter what we did, as far as privacy allows.
