# Ihsaanly — Support

## Something is wrong

**More → Your data → Send a diagnostic report.** It lists what it contains
before anything is sent, and lets you read the whole report first. It carries
enough for us to reproduce what you saw.

## Common questions

**Why doesn't the app show prayer times?**
On purpose. It tells you which part of the day you are in, and you tell it when
you have prayed. A window that is three minutes out is invisible; a clock that
is three minutes out contradicts your mosque.

**The Hijri date disagrees with my mosque.**
**More → Hijri date** lets you shift it by up to two days. Dates in the app are
calculated, and calculation and local sighting often differ.

**Why does it say "expected" next to fasting days?**
Because they are calculated, not sighted. On a day where readings differ — the
ninth of Dhul Hijjah locally versus the day of standing in Makkah — the app
shows both rather than choosing for you.

**I don't want to share my location.**
You never have to. Pick your city instead; everything works.

**Why so few notifications?**
Two or three a day by default, and none for the obligatory prayers unless you
ask. You almost certainly have an adhan app already.

**I marked a prayer by mistake.**
Tap it again.

**Who checked the religious content?**
Every item shows its source and its grading, and names the grader where the
collection does not carry its own. Once a reviewer has approved the content they
are credited in the app, on the About screen; until then that screen says the
content is awaiting review, and says so plainly.
