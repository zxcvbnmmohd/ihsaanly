Ihsaanly shows you the sunnah that fits the moment: the morning adhkar after Fajr, the two rak'ah after Dhuhr, the dua as you leave the house, the fast that begins on Thursday.

YOU MARK THE PRAYER. THE SUNNAH APPEARS.
Tap Dhuhr when you have prayed it, and the dhikr that follows is waiting. The app shows the part of the day you are in, not a clock time, so it never competes with your mosque's timetable.

EVERY ITEM SHOWS ITS SOURCE
Each act shows where it comes from: the verse, or the hadith collection with its reference and grading, and who graded it where the collection does not carry its own grading. Where scholars differ, the app says so rather than choosing for you. Every item also explains in plain words why it matters and how to do it.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• Morning and evening adhkar, and the tasbih and Ayat al-Kursi after prayer
• The sunnah prayers before and after the obligatory prayers, Witr and Duha
• Duas for leaving and coming home, entering the masjid, eating, sleeping and waking, travelling, riding, going up and going down
• Fasting days: Mondays and Thursdays, the White Days, Ashura, Arafah, the six days of Shawwal and the first ten days of Dhul Hijjah
• Arabic, transliteration and translation together, with how many times to say it
• A library you can search by name or by situation, and a glossary of the terms it uses

KNOW WHAT IS COMING
Fasting days appear in advance, marked "expected", because they are calculated rather than sighted. Shift the Hijri date by up to two days so it agrees with your community.

LEARN IT, THEN LET IT GO
Learn a dua by hiding the transliteration, then the translation. Mark it as known and the app stops reminding you about it.

CALM BY DESIGN
Two or three reminders a day, not fifteen, and nothing during quiet hours. No streaks, no scores, nothing that turns worship into a game. Dismiss anything without explaining yourself. Prayers and fasts you owe are kept as a simple count to make up, never a list of dates.

START SMALL
Choose Just starting, Essentials or Everything when you set up, then add or remove anything in the Library whenever you like.

WIDGETS
Home screen widgets keep it one glance away: what is relevant right now, the next prayer, today's prayers, the Hijri date, what is up next and what else today holds, quick duas, a dua of the day, what you have to make up, and the days coming up.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising. It works fully offline, and fully without granting a single permission: decline location and choose your city instead. Your practice is recorded on your device and stays there. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

HONEST ABOUT ITSELF
The content is drafted and awaiting review by a named scholar, and the app's About screen says so. English is the original; the other languages are drafts awaiting review by qualified speakers.

Ihsaanly is free, with nothing locked and no advertising.
